Text caption

Tracking is the space between characters, in dots. Each font starts at the spacing measured from its frames. Proportional spacing gives each letter only the width it needs and kerns awkward pairs like LT, TA and AV; switch it off for the strict generator grid. Weight thickens the upright strokes without touching the horizontal lines. Dot gap separates the dots for an LED matrix look. Scanlines splits each row of dots into slabs with thin black gaps, as in the Type 03 reference; it works on every font.

Video signal look

Edge colour is the cross-colour on stroke edges: lavender and blue where a stroke starts, yellow-green where it ends. Colour noise is the soft pink and green tinting inside the white strokes.

Animation

Static only lives on and around the lettering: streaks run along the text rows and thin out with distance, smear drags the letters into fading trails, and the black stays black. It is mixed in before the video look, so it softens and fringes like the text. Tearing slides bands of the text sideways; breakup eats dashes out of the letters. Playback renders at half size to keep it smooth; Save PNG always renders full size. The clip is saved as a WebM video.

Font glyph editor

Click or drag across the dots to switch them on and off. Edited characters are outlined; your edits stay in this browser.

Output

MPU saves at 1200×1000 (300×250 at 4×). Strip sizes itself to the text: 100 pixels of black around it, at least 1920 wide. Text is centred both ways; empty lines at the end are ignored.
